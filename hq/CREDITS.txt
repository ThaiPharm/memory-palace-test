Bathroom fixtures, floor lamps, rugs, fridges, people and crowns have no matching realistic CC0 model here yet;
on High/Ultra they use improved procedural shapes (rounded edges, PBR materials, soft contact shadows).
Door and window frames are modelled in the house, not loaded as assets.

All models were re-compressed with glTF-Transform (meshopt geometry, WebP textures at 512 px, some simplified).
